HR DEPARTMENT INTERNAL USE

Attendance and Leave Records:
The standard work week is 40 hours. Employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave.
Medical leave requires a physician's note if exceeding three consecutive business days.

Performance Review Schedule:
Annual reviews are conducted every January. Mid-year check-ins occur in July.
Ratings are based on a 1-5 scale, where 5 represents "Exemplary Performance."

Payroll Information:
Salaries are disbursed on the 28th of every month. Direct deposit setup takes two billing cycles to activate.
